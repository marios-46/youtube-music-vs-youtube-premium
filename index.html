YouTube Music vs YouTube Premium: What's the Difference?

Both subscriptions improve the YouTube experience, but they are designed for different listening and viewing habits. Discover what each plan includes, how their features compare, and which option makes sense for you.

Watch. Listen. Discover. A practical guide to YouTube memberships
Category: Technology & Streaming Topic: Subscription Comparison Reading: Beginner Friendly

YouTube has developed into more than a place to watch short clips and entertainment videos. People now use the platform to discover new artists, listen to albums, follow podcasts, watch educational content, and explore music videos from around the world. With so many different ways to use the service, choosing the right subscription can sometimes become confusing.

Two membership options that people frequently compare are YouTube Music Premium and YouTube Premium. Their names sound similar, and both offer benefits related to music playback. However, they do not provide exactly the same experience. One is designed mainly for music listeners, while the other combines music benefits with additional features for regular YouTube videos.

The short answer: YouTube Music Premium focuses on ad-free music listening, background playback, and offline music downloads inside the YouTube Music app. Full YouTube Premium includes those music benefits and adds advantages for watching regular YouTube videos, including eligible ad-free viewing and supported video downloads.

This guide explains the differences in simple language. It covers the features, apps, subscription benefits, limitations, and situations in which one membership may be more useful than the other.

1. Understanding YouTube Music and YouTube Premium

Before comparing the subscriptions, it helps to understand that YouTube and YouTube Music are connected services with different purposes. The regular YouTube platform is built around video discovery. Its library includes creator channels, tutorials, entertainment, documentaries, livestreams, interviews, music videos, and many other categories.

YouTube Music is a dedicated music-listening service. Its interface is organised around songs, artists, albums, playlists, music videos, remixes, and music discovery. Rather than browsing a general video feed, listeners can use music-focused sections to find tracks and organise what they want to hear.

The subscriptions follow this distinction. YouTube Music Premium adds paid listening benefits to the music service. YouTube Premium is a broader membership that improves the regular video experience and includes YouTube Music Premium as part of the package in supported markets.

This relationship is the most important point to remember when comparing the two. You do not need to purchase both memberships separately to get the full set of standard YouTube Premium benefits.

2. What Is YouTube Music Premium?

YouTube Music Premium is a paid subscription for people who primarily use YouTube to listen to music. It builds on the free, ad-supported YouTube Music experience by adding features that make listening more convenient. These benefits are especially useful when someone listens throughout the day, switches between applications, or wants music available without a continuous internet connection.

The service provides access to music listening features within the YouTube Music app. Users can explore songs, albums, playlists, artist pages, music videos, covers, remixes, and other available recordings. The catalogue and availability of individual tracks can vary by region and licensing arrangements.

How music downloads work

Offline downloads allow subscribers to save supported tracks and other eligible music content through the YouTube Music application. Once downloaded, the content can be played without streaming it again over the internet. This can reduce mobile data usage and make listening more practical when travelling.

Downloads remain managed inside the supported app experience. They should not be confused with unrestricted audio files that can be copied to any music player. Access is subject to YouTube's membership, content, and offline playback conditions.

Is YouTube Music Premium only for songs?

Music is the main focus, but the service also includes podcasts and other audio-related content where available. However, podcast features can follow different rules from music playback. For example, some podcasts may include creator-read sponsorships or promotional messages. A paid music subscription does not necessarily remove every promotion within podcast content.

3. What Is YouTube Premium?

YouTube Premium is the broader paid membership for people who regularly watch videos and listen to music on YouTube. It includes the main YouTube Music Premium benefits in supported markets, along with additional features for the regular YouTube platform.

The membership is connected to a Google account. After subscribing through an official channel and signing in with the correct account, eligible benefits become available across supported YouTube apps and devices. The same account can be used on compatible phones, tablets, computers, and televisions.

Important YouTube Premium benefits

  • Ad-free viewing: Eligible regular YouTube videos can be watched without standard platform advertisements before or during playback.
  • Video background play: Supported videos can continue playing in the background on compatible mobile applications.
  • Offline video downloads: Save supported videos and playlists through the official YouTube app for offline viewing.
  • YouTube Music Premium: Enjoy the music-focused membership benefits as part of the full Premium package in supported markets.
  • Additional viewing features: Certain extra playback and account features may be available depending on the device, content, and region.

Why video background play matters

Background play is useful when a video contains information that does not require constant attention to the screen. For example, someone listening to a long interview, language lesson, lecture, or discussion may want to check messages or open another app without stopping playback.

With supported Premium playback, the audio can continue after leaving the YouTube mobile app or locking the device. Feature availability depends on the application and content type. YouTube's documentation also identifies restrictions, including the Shorts player.

Watching videos offline

YouTube Premium also supports offline downloads for eligible videos. This is useful for people who commute, travel, or study in places where a reliable internet connection is not always available. A user can download supported content in advance and watch it later through the official app.

Offline downloads are not permanent copies that can be freely moved between devices. Availability may depend on licensing, regional restrictions, the content owner, and the service's offline access rules.

5. Does YouTube Premium Include YouTube Music Premium?

Yes. Full YouTube Premium includes YouTube Music Premium benefits in supported markets. This means subscribers can use the dedicated YouTube Music app for eligible ad-free music playback, background listening, and offline downloads without purchasing a separate Music Premium membership.

This arrangement is useful for people who switch between video and music content. They can use regular YouTube for creator channels, documentaries, tutorials, and other videos, then open YouTube Music when they want a more focused music interface.

It is still important to check the exact membership details shown during checkout. Plan availability, eligibility, and included features can vary by country or account type.

6. Understanding the Two Apps

Although the services are connected, the two applications are designed around different activities. Understanding their interfaces can make it easier to decide which one belongs in your everyday routine.

The regular YouTube app

The main YouTube app is built for discovering and watching videos. Its homepage can display recommendations based on viewing activity, subscriptions, and other signals. Users can search for channels, follow creators, browse playlists, watch livestreams, and explore different categories.

This makes the regular app suitable for people who enjoy a wide variety of content rather than focusing only on music. A student might use it for tutorials, a technology enthusiast might follow product reviews, and another viewer might watch documentaries or entertainment channels.

The YouTube Music app

YouTube Music has a more specialised interface. It focuses on music discovery, listening history, artists, albums, playlists, and personalised recommendations. Users can move between songs and music videos where both versions are available.

The app can be a better fit for someone who opens a streaming service primarily to play music rather than browse general video content. Its listening-oriented controls help keep music discovery and playback at the centre of the experience.

Practical tip: You do not have to choose only one app. A full YouTube Premium membership can provide benefits in both the regular YouTube app and YouTube Music, using the same eligible Google account.

7. Free YouTube Music vs YouTube Music Premium

YouTube Music can be used without a paid membership. The free experience gives users access to a music catalogue supported by advertising and applicable playback rules. This means someone can explore the service before deciding whether a subscription is useful.

The paid version adds benefits such as ad-free music, background playback, and music downloads. These features can make a difference for people who listen frequently or want to keep music playing while doing other tasks.

Not every feature is exclusive to paid members. For example, some podcast listening options and podcast downloads may be available without a Music Premium subscription. The rules for podcasts can differ from those for songs and music videos.

8. Plans, Availability, and Pricing

YouTube offers different membership options, and the plans displayed to a user can depend on their country, eligibility, and account circumstances. Individual, family, student, and other plan types may be offered in selected markets. Not every option is available everywhere.

YouTube Music Premium and YouTube Premium also have separate subscription options in markets where both are offered. Since prices and plan conditions can change, it is better to check the official membership page rather than rely on an old price listed in an article or social media post.

What should you check before subscribing?

  • Which membership options are available in your country?
  • Does the plan include individual or multi-person access?
  • Are you eligible for a student or promotional offer?
  • What is the renewal date and billing frequency?
  • Which payment method and billing provider will be used?
  • Does the selected plan include the features you actually need?

Always review the final checkout screen before confirming a subscription. This helps avoid misunderstandings about recurring payments, trial periods, plan restrictions, or renewal conditions.

9. Which Membership Should You Choose?

There is no single answer that works for every user. The right choice depends on how you use YouTube. Think about the content you consume most often and which limitations you would actually like to remove.

Choose YouTube Music Premium if you mainly listen to music

This option may be suitable if you spend most of your time listening to songs, albums, playlists, remixes, and music videos inside YouTube Music. Its background listening and offline downloads can be particularly helpful for people who use music throughout the day.

It may also be a reasonable choice if you are already happy with the regular YouTube viewing experience and do not need additional video-related membership benefits.

Choose YouTube Premium if you watch videos and listen to music

The broader membership may be more suitable if you frequently use both YouTube services. In addition to the music benefits, it adds eligible ad-free video viewing, supported offline video downloads, and background playback for regular YouTube videos on compatible mobile apps.

It can be useful for people who watch tutorials, interviews, educational lessons, entertainment, and long-form discussions while also listening to music through the dedicated music app.

A simple way to decide

Mostly music? Consider YouTube Music Premium.

Music and regular videos? Consider full YouTube Premium.

Not sure you need paid features? Try the free services first and identify which limitations affect your routine.

10. Official Apps, Modified APKs, and Account Safety

Android users may come across websites discussing modified versions of YouTube applications. These packages are often described as modified APKs. An APK is an Android application package, and a modified version is software that has been changed from its original distribution.

It is important to understand the difference between a third-party application package and an official YouTube membership. A modified application does not automatically create a valid subscription, provide official account benefits, or become an authorised Google product.

People researching this topic may encounter information about YouTube Premium APK. This is a third-party resource, not YouTube's official membership or Google Play download page. Readers should independently assess the source, software permissions, update practices, and possible security implications before installing any unofficial package.

Why should users be careful with unofficial APK files?

  • Unknown changes: It may be difficult to determine exactly what has been changed in a modified application.
  • Permission concerns: Some packages may request permissions that are not necessary for their stated purpose.
  • Update uncertainty: Unofficial packages may not receive updates through the same trusted channels as official apps.
  • Account privacy: Users should never share Google passwords or sensitive account details with unofficial services.
  • Compatibility issues: Changes to the official platform can cause modified applications to stop working correctly.

For recognised membership benefits, account management, billing, and official support, use YouTube's own services and supported application channels. A third-party APK should not be treated as equivalent to a paid membership.

11. How to Check Which Membership You Have

If you are unsure whether your account has YouTube Music Premium or full YouTube Premium, check the membership information connected to your Google account. This is more reliable than judging the subscription from the app icon or the features visible on one device.

  1. Open YouTube or visit the official YouTube website.
  2. Sign in using the Google account associated with your subscription.
  3. Open your profile or account menu.
  4. Find the purchases and memberships section.
  5. Review the active plan and its included benefits.

If you subscribed through an app store or another billing provider, some payment and cancellation details may need to be managed through that provider. Check the account's billing information before changing or cancelling a plan.

12. Common Questions About the Two Subscriptions

1. Is YouTube Music Premium the same as YouTube Premium?

No. YouTube Music Premium focuses on music listening inside YouTube Music. Full YouTube Premium includes those music benefits and adds features for eligible regular YouTube videos.

2. Does YouTube Premium include YouTube Music Premium?

Yes. Full YouTube Premium includes YouTube Music Premium benefits in supported markets, so a separate Music Premium subscription is generally not needed for those benefits.

3. Can I listen to music with my phone screen off?

YouTube Music Premium supports background music playback on compatible mobile devices. Full YouTube Premium also provides supported background playback for regular YouTube videos.

4. Does YouTube Music Premium remove ads from regular YouTube videos?

No. Its ad-free benefits apply to eligible music content in YouTube Music. Removing standard advertisements from eligible regular YouTube videos is a benefit of full YouTube Premium.

5. Can I download songs with YouTube Music Premium?

Yes. Subscribers can download supported music for offline listening through the YouTube Music app, subject to the service's download and access conditions.

6. Can I download regular YouTube videos with Music Premium?

YouTube Music Premium does not include the general offline video download benefits provided by full YouTube Premium.

7. Can I use both YouTube and YouTube Music apps?

Yes. Both apps can be used with the same Google account. A full YouTube Premium membership provides benefits across supported YouTube and YouTube Music experiences.

8. Are music videos included in YouTube Music?

YouTube Music includes music videos where available, alongside songs, albums, playlists, remixes, and other music content.

9. Does YouTube Premium remove every type of promotion?

No. Creator-read sponsorships, product placements, and promotional messages included directly in content may still appear. Some live events and other experiences can also have separate advertising conditions.

10. Are the subscription prices the same in every country?

No. Membership prices, available plans, payment options, and eligibility conditions can vary by country. Check the official membership page for the options shown to your account.

11. Can I cancel a YouTube membership?

Memberships can be managed through the relevant official account or billing provider. Review the renewal date and cancellation conditions before making changes.

12. Which subscription is better for students?

It depends on whether the student mainly listens to music or also watches regular YouTube videos. Eligible students may have access to specific plan options in supported markets, subject to verification requirements.

13. Final Thoughts: YouTube Music vs YouTube Premium

YouTube Music Premium and YouTube Premium serve related but different purposes. Music Premium is designed for people who want a more convenient music-listening experience, with eligible ad-free playback, background listening, and offline downloads. It is a focused option for users whose main activity is listening to songs and exploring music content.

Full YouTube Premium offers a wider package. It includes YouTube Music Premium benefits in supported markets and adds features for the regular YouTube platform, such as eligible ad-free video playback, supported video downloads, and background play on compatible mobile apps.

The best choice depends on your daily habits. If music is your priority, Music Premium may provide the features you need. If you regularly watch videos and listen to music, the broader Premium membership may be more practical. Compare the available plans and conditions in your region before making a decision.
